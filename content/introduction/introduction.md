# Introduction

## How did the Snurch begin?

The Snurch was born as a joke born from the "Take me to church" Parody "Take me to Snurch" (See below). Over time the idea of the Snurch became more concrete and has now led to the writing of this Snible.

## What is the Snurch about?

The founding Principle of the Snurch is **"Be good, do good"**. It is a rather short statement and will be expanded upon but it is sufficient for a start, try to be good (to yourself) and do good (for others). This is inspired by Immanuel Kants work "Categorical Imperative" and its most famous quote "Act only in accord to that maxim whereby you can at the same time will that it should become universal law.".
